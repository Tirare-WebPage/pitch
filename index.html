THE PROBLEM · THE PERSON BEHIND THE VEHICLE

A broken pedal can cost a day's income.

We saw a cargo-tricycle worker keep hauling goods even with a broken pedal.

For most of us, a broken pedal means stop and repair it. For someone who earns with that vehicle, stopping can mean losing a day's income.

These tricycles are not just old machines. They are how people earn a living.
OUR QUESTION · Can we make the vehicle easier to work with without asking its owner to replace it?

THE MECHANICAL PROBLEM

The motor pushes back on the frame.

The motor turns the axle.

The axle pushes back on the motor with the same turning force in the opposite direction.

That push travels through the motor mount and into the frame.

We have to design for the tricycle that is already there.
MOTOR TURNS AXLE↔AXLE PUSHES BACK

Frames differ in material, condition and past repairs. The mount and frame still need engineering review and testing.
