STRUCTURAL DYNAMICS

A motor pushes back.
The frame must take it.

Motor torque acts on the axle. An equal and opposite reaction passes through the motor housing and mount into the frame.

Old frames vary in construction, condition and repairs. The mount and frame need engineering review and testing.

Proportional assistance does not eliminate reaction loads.

ARCHITECTURE · PEDAL INPUT + MOTOR ASSIST06 / 22

DRIVETRAIN INNOVATION

We changed the interface.

E-bike conversions bolt cadence rings or bottom-bracket strain gauges beside the cranks. Each adds parts beside the crank.

MRII replaces the driven sprocket itself. It sits in the mechanical power path from pedal chain to live axle.

Pedal crank→Chain→MRII→Drive axle

MRII senses effort inside the torque-transmitting element, avoiding a separate crank-side sensing bracket.
